PANOOJA CUSTOMER CARE

Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Order Processing & Dispatch

All orders placed on PANOOJA are verified and dispatched within 24 business hours from our central fulfillment studio. Orders placed on Sundays or national holidays will be processed on the next business day.

2. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery

We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden checkout fees:

  • Orders of ₹799 or above: FREE Express Shipping anywhere in India.
  • Orders below ₹799: A standard flat shipping charge of ₹79 applies nationwide.

3. Delivery Timelines

We partner with premier courier networks (including BlueDart, Delhivery, and Xpressbees) to ensure prompt delivery:

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 2 to 4 business days.
  • Rest of India: 3 to 5 business days.

4. Live Tracking & Delivery Updates

As soon as your package is dispatched, you will receive real-time SMS and WhatsApp notifications containing your AWB tracking link. You can track your shipment journey directly from dispatch to doorstep delivery.

5. Discreet & Secure Packaging

Every piece of PANOOJA fine jewellery is housed in a rigid, tamper-evident presentation box cushioned with protective inserts to ensure safe transit.
