DATA PROTECTION & TRUST

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

PANOOJA collects information necessary to fulfill your jewellery purchases and provide seamless customer support:

  • Contact & Delivery Details: Your name, delivery address, postal PIN code, mobile/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, order reference ID, selected delivery method, and payment confirmation status.
  • Account Information: When signing in with Google, we receive your verified email address, full name, and avatar solely to personalize your shopping experience.

2. Payment Security

Orders are confirmed on WhatsApp and paid by UPI. We never ask for your UPI PIN or card details.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely for:

  • Fulfilling and delivering your orders.
  • Sending shipping tracking links and delivery status updates via SMS and WhatsApp.
  • Responding to customer support inquiries and replacement requests.
  • Delivering requested product updates or storefront offers if you opted in to our newsletter (unsubscribe anytime).

4. Zero Spam & No Third-Party Sales

We respect your privacy completely. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or marketing brokers under any circumstances.

5. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

PANOOJA uses browser local storage and essential session technologies to keep the store working smoothly:

  • Shopping Bag & Coupon Preferences: Stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so your cart items and applied promo codes stay saved between page visits.
  • Google Sign-In: Used only when you choose to sign in with Google to pre-fill your name and email at checkout.
  • Online Payment Provider: When online payments are active, our payment gateway (Razorpay) may set strictly necessary cookies/session tokens to process and verify transactions securely.

6. Google Sign-In Data & WhatsApp Orders

Google Sign-In: Signing in with Google is optional. When you sign in, we only access basic profile information you authorize (name and verified email address) to simplify checkout. You can sign out at any time.

WhatsApp Orders: When you place an order or contact us via WhatsApp (+91 7984528870), your order summary, name, phone number, and delivery address are shared directly in your WhatsApp chat with us and are also subject to WhatsApp/Meta’s privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

We retain order, invoice, and support correspondence records only for as long as needed to fulfill your order, handle replacements/refunds, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable Indian tax, accounting, and e-commerce laws. Local cart data remains on your device until cleared by you.

8. Your Rights Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

As a customer in India, you have rights regarding your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), including the right to:

  • Request summary/access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate personal data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data where retention is no longer required by law.
  • Withdraw previously given consent (such as newsletter updates) at any time.
  • Seek grievance redressal regarding how your data is handled.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@panooja.com or contact our Grievance Officer below.
