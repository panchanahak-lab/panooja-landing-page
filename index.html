PANOOJA jewellery is designed to resist water and sweat with proper care, making it suitable for everyday wear.

How fast is PANOOJA delivery across India?+

Dispatched within 24 hours · Delivery in 2–5 business days via DTDC, Delhivery, BlueDart, or The Professional Couriers. You will receive live SMS and WhatsApp tracking updates once your parcel ships.

Do you offer free shipping?+

Yes! We offer FREE Shipping on all orders above ₹799 across India. For orders below ₹799, a flat shipping charge of ₹79 is applied at checkout.

Order via WhatsApp — we confirm stock and share UPI payment details, and dispatch once payment is received.

What is your return & exchange policy?+

We offer a 7-day replacement for damaged, defective, or wrong items. Simply reach out to our WhatsApp concierge with your order number and an uncut unboxing video, and we will assist you promptly.

Is PANOOJA jewellery suitable for sensitive skin?+

Our jewellery is made with 316L stainless steel, which is generally well tolerated by sensitive skin.
