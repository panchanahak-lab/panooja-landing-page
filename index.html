FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What makes PANOOJA jewellery anti-tarnish and waterproof?+

We craft our jewellery using high-grade 316L surgical stainless steel coated with vacuum PVD (Physical Vapour Deposition) real 18K gold. Unlike traditional flash-plating that tarnishes within days, our PVD bond is waterproof, sweatproof, and highly scratch-resistant for long-lasting daily wear.

Can I wear PANOOJA jewellery in the shower, pool or gym?+

Yes! Every piece is tested to resist water, sweat, and moisture. You can comfortably wear your hoops, rings, and chains in the shower, during workouts, and throughout your daily routine without discoloration.
