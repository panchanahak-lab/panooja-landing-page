PANOOJA Self-Hosted Product Images Folder
=========================================

Upload product photos here (recommended: WebP format, max 1200px wide, under 150 KB each).

Example filenames:
  products/cat-eye-1.webp
  products/cat-eye-2.webp
  products/sea-shell-pendant-1.webp

How to link them in your Google Sheet:
  In the "Direct Image URL (Auto)" or "Direct Image URL 2 (Auto)" column, enter:
    /products/cat-eye-1.webp

Google Drive links continue to work as usual alongside /products/ paths.
