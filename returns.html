PANOOJA CUSTOMER CARE

Replacement, Refunds & Cancellations Policy

1. 7-Day Replacement (damaged/defective items)

At PANOOJA, every order is checked before dispatch. In the rare event that an item arrives damaged in transit, with a manufacturing defect, or differs from what was ordered, we offer a 7-Day Replacement (damaged/defective items) within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a replacement:

  • The request must be reported within 7 calendar days of parcel delivery.
  • The product must be unused, unworn, and in its original presentation packaging with any tags intact.
  • A clear, uncut unboxing video (showing the unopened parcel label and opening the box in one continuous shot) and clear photographs of the damaged, defective, or wrong item must be provided to our support team.

3. How to Initiate a Replacement

To request a replacement, reach out to our Customer Care team via WhatsApp (+91 7984528870) or email (support@panooja.com) with:

  • Your PANOOJA Order ID / Payment Reference.
  • A brief description of the issue.
  • Your uncut unboxing video and clear photos.

Our team typically reviews replacement requests within 24–48 hours. Once approved, we will arrange a reverse pickup or dispatch a replacement piece at no additional shipping cost.

4. Hygiene & Non-Returnable Scenarios

Due to hygiene standards for personal wear jewellery, items that have been worn, altered, or damaged due to improper handling (such as bending, chemical exposure, or drops) are not eligible for replacement or return. Change-of-mind returns are not offered.

5. Refunds & Cancellations

Pre-Dispatch Cancellations: You may request to cancel an order before it is dispatched from our Surat studio by messaging our Customer Care WhatsApp (+91 7984528870) or emailing support@panooja.com with your Order ID. Verified pre-dispatch cancellations receive a 100% full refund.

Post-Dispatch Cancellations: Once an order has been handed over to our courier partner and dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

Failed or Returned Deliveries (RTO): If a prepaid parcel is returned to us due to repeated failed delivery attempts, an incorrect/incomplete address, or refusal at the doorstep, we can either re-dispatch the parcel (customer-borne re-shipping fee may apply) or process a refund after deducting forward and return courier charges.

When Refunds Are Issued: Refunds are issued for approved pre-dispatch cancellations, out-of-stock items that cannot be fulfilled, or approved damaged/defective/wrong-item claims where a replacement piece is unavailable.

Refund Method & Timeline: Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method (for online payments) or to your verified UPI ID / bank account (for WhatsApp/UPI orders) within 5–7 working days of approval.
