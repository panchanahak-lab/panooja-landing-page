PANOOJA CUSTOMER CARE

Returns & Exchange Policy

1. 7-Day Exchange & Replacement Guarantee

At PANOOJA, every piece of jewellery is thoroughly hand-inspected before shipment. However, in the rare event that an item arrives damaged in transit, with a manufacturing defect, or differs from what was ordered, we offer a dedicated 7-Day Replacement Guarantee from the date of delivery.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a replacement or exchange:

  • The request must be reported within 7 calendar days of parcel delivery.
  • The product must be unused, unworn, and in its original presentation packaging with any tags intact.
  • A clear, uncut unboxing video or clear photographs showing the defect or transit damage must be provided to our concierge team.

3. How to Initiate a Replacement

To request a prompt exchange, simply reach out to our Customer Concierge via WhatsApp or email with:

  • Your PANOOJA Order ID / Payment Reference.
  • A brief description of the issue.
  • Unboxing photo or video footage.

Our concierge team typically reviews and approves replacement requests within 24 business hours. A reverse pickup will be arranged or a replacement parcel will be immediately dispatched upon verification.

4. Hygiene & Non-Returnable Scenarios

Due to strict hygiene standards for personal wear jewellery, items that have been worn, altered, or damaged due to improper customer handling (such as intentional excessive bending or exposure to harsh industrial abrasives) cannot be accepted for exchange.
